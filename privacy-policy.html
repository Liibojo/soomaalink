Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

SoomaaLink ("we", "us") is a short-video social app for the Somali community. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: username, display name, profile photo, and your email address, phone number and password used to sign up.
  • Content you create: videos, captions, hashtags, comments, likes, saved items and messages.
  • Usage information: videos you watch, accounts you follow, searches, and how you use the app.
  • Device information: device model, operating system, language, IP address and crash logs.
  • Live features: if you use Live, the video and audio you broadcast and the comments sent during it.

We do not knowingly collect precise location.

3. How we use your information

  • To run the app, show your content and recommend videos.
  • To let you follow, comment, message and share.
  • To keep the community safe, review reports and remove harmful content.
  • To fix bugs, prevent fraud and improve the app.
  • To respond to your questions and legal requests.

4. Sharing your information

Public content (videos, comments, profile) can be seen by other users unless you set your account to Private. We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers who host or support the app (for example cloud storage), and with authorities when required by law or to protect safety.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Private account: you can make your account private in Settings.
  • Block users: you can block accounts so they cannot interact with you.
  • Comments and messages: you can control who can comment and message you.
  • Delete content: you can delete your videos at any time.
  • Delete your account: you can delete your account and data inside the app (Settings > Delete account), or by emailing boyyare432@gmail.com. We delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except what we must keep by law or for safety.

If you are in France or the EU/EEA, you also have rights under the GDPR: to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and to object to its use. Contact us at the email below. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active. After deletion we remove it within the period above.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure.

8. Children

SoomaaLink is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children below this age. If you believe a child has joined, contact boyyare432@gmail.com and we will remove the account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for major changes, notify you in the app.
