Soomaali

SoomaaLink waa app lagu daawado muuqaallo Soomaali ah. Bogga sirtu wuxuu sharxayaa xogta aan isticmaalno.

1. Xogta aan ururinno

SoomaaLink ma lahan akoon (login), mana ururiso magacaaga, email-kaaga, lambarkaaga taleefanka, goobtaada ama aqoonsiyada kale. Mana iibino xog.

2. Xogta ku jirta taleefankaaga

Waxyaabaha soo socda waxay ku keydsan yihiin taleefankaaga oo keliya (ma tagaan server-ka): muuqaallada aad jeceshahay (like), kuwa aad follow-garayso, muuqaallada aad ku darto, muuqaallada aad qarisay/block-gareysay, iyo in aad aqbashay Shuruudaha.

3. Adeegyada dhinac saddexaad (YouTube)

Muuqaallada waxaa ciyaara YouTube (player-ka rasmiga ah). Marka aad daawato, YouTube/Google waxay u shaqeeyaan sida xeerarkooda: policies.google.com/privacy iyo YouTube Terms. SoomaaLink kuma xukumo xogta YouTube ururiso.

4. Nuxurka dadka (UGC) iyo Report

Muuqaal walba waxaad ku soo sheegi kartaa badhanka ⚑ Report. Waxaad sidoo kale qari kartaa muuqaal ama block-gareyn kartaa milkiilaha. Waxaan ka saari karnaa nuxur ku xad gudba Shuruudaha. Nuxur galmo, rabshad, nacayb, xadgudub carruur ama xad gudub xuquuq lama oggola.

5. Tirtirka xogtaada

Gudaha app-ka: Profile → Tirtir xogtayda. Taasi waxay tirtirtaa xogta ku keydsan taleefankaaga. Waad sidoo kale tirtiri kartaa adigoo app-ka ka saaraya taleefankaaga. Haddii aad rabto in muuqaal laga saaro SoomaaLink, nala soo xiriir.

6. Carruurta

SoomaaLink ma ururiso xog shaqsi ah oo carruur ah. Waalidku waa inuu kormeeraa waxa ay carruurtu daawadaan.

7. Isbeddellada

Bogga waan cusboonaysiin karnaa. Taariikhda kore ayaa muujinaysa cusboonaysiinta ugu dambeysay.

English

SoomaaLink is an app for watching Somali videos. This page explains what data we use.

1. Data we collect

SoomaaLink has no accounts or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location or other identifiers, and we do not sell data.

2. Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device (it is not sent to a server): videos you liked, creators you follow, videos you add, videos you hide or block, and whether you accepted the Terms.

3. Third-party services (YouTube)

Videos are played by the official YouTube embedded player. YouTube/Google handle data under their own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and YouTube Terms of Service.

4. User-generated content and reporting

You can report any video with the ⚑ Report button, hide a video, or block its creator. We may remove content that breaks our Terms. Sexual content, violence, hate, child exploitation and copyright infringement are not allowed.

5. Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Tirtir xogtayda (Delete my data). This erases the data stored on your device. You can also uninstall the app. To request removal of a video from SoomaaLink, contact us.

6. Children

SoomaaLink does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents should supervise what children watch.

7. Changes

We may update this page. The date above shows the latest update.
